# Returns, Damage and Claims Policy

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-CS-003 | **Version:** 2.4 | **Owner:** Customer Service Manager

## 1. Reporting damage or shortage
- **Visible damage or shortage:** note it on the proof of delivery when the goods arrive, then report it in the customer portal within 3 working days.
- **Hidden (concealed) damage:** report within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- Claims reported after these time limits are not accepted, except where the law requires it.

## 2. What to include in a claim
- consignment or tracking number
- photos of the damage and the outer packaging
- a copy of the commercial invoice showing the value of the goods
- the signed proof of delivery

## 3. Liability limits
Unless the customer bought extra cover, Meridian's liability is limited to:
- UK road: £1,300 per tonne of goods lost or damaged
- International road: as set by the CMR Convention
- Air and sea: as set by the relevant international conventions

Customers can buy Declared Value Cover at booking for goods worth more than these limits.

## 4. Claim timeline
- Acknowledgement within 2 working days.
- Investigation completed within 15 working days.
- Approved claims paid within 30 days of approval.

## 5. Returns (reverse logistics)
- Customers book a return in the portal and receive a Returns Authorisation (RA) number.
- Goods arriving without an RA number are held for 14 days and then returned to the sender at their cost.
- Returned goods are inspected within 48 hours and graded A (resell), B (refurbish) or C (dispose).

## 6. Disputes
If a customer disagrees with a claim decision, they can ask for a review by the Customer Service Manager within 20 working days of the decision.
