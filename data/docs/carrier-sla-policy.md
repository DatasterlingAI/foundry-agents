# Carrier Service Level Agreement Policy

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-OPS-001 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Owner:** Head of Transport | **Review date:** March 2027

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the service levels Meridian expects from all third-party road, sea and air carriers, and how performance is measured and enforced.

## 2. Delivery time targets
| Service | Region | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Next-day road | UK mainland | Delivered by 17:00 next working day |
| Same-day road | Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah | Delivered within 6 hours of collection |
| Standard road | UK mainland | 2 working days |
| GCC cross-border road | UAE to KSA, Oman | 4 working days including customs |
| Sea freight (FCL) | Jebel Ali to Felixstowe | 24 days port to port |
| Air freight | DXB to LHR | 2 days airport to airport |

Scottish Highlands, Northern Ireland and UK islands get one extra working day.

## 3. Performance measures
- **On-time delivery (OTD):** at least 97% each month.
- **Proof of delivery (POD):** electronic POD uploaded within 2 hours of delivery.
- **Damage rate:** below 0.3% of consignments per month.
- **Collection reliability:** at least 98% of collections within the agreed 2-hour window.

## 4. Penalties and credits
- If monthly OTD falls between 95% and 96.9%, the carrier credits 2% of that month's invoice.
- If OTD falls below 95%, the credit rises to 5% and a performance improvement plan is required within 10 working days.
- Two consecutive months below 95% allows Meridian to end the contract with 30 days' notice.

## 5. Reporting
Carriers send a monthly scorecard by the 5th working day of the following month. The Transport team reviews scorecards in the monthly carrier review meeting.

## 6. Escalation
Missed same-day deliveries in the UAE are escalated to the Duty Transport Manager immediately. All other failures are logged in the carrier portal within 24 hours.
