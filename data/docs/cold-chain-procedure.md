# Cold Chain Handling Procedure

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-OPS-007 | **Version:** 1.6 | **Owner:** Quality Manager

## 1. Temperature ranges
| Product type | Required range |
|---|---|
| Chilled food | 0 °C to 5 °C |
| Pharmaceuticals (cold) | 2 °C to 8 °C |
| Pharmaceuticals (controlled room temperature) | 15 °C to 25 °C |
| Frozen | -25 °C to -18 °C |

## 2. Receiving
- Check the trailer temperature record before unloading. Reject the load if the record shows readings outside range for more than 30 minutes in total, unless the customer's Quality team approves acceptance in writing.
- Chilled and frozen goods must move from the dock to the cold store within 20 minutes.
- Probe a sample of 1 in every 20 pallets and record the reading.

## 3. Storage
- Cold store temperatures are logged every 5 minutes by data loggers.
- An alarm triggers if the temperature stays out of range for 10 minutes. The on-call engineer must respond within 30 minutes.
- Pharmaceutical stock is stored in a separate, access-controlled area.

## 4. Temperature excursions
An excursion is any reading outside the required range.
1. Quarantine the affected stock and label it "On hold – temperature excursion".
2. Download the logger data.
3. Inform the customer's Quality contact within 2 hours.
4. Do not release or destroy stock until the customer gives written instructions.

## 5. Transport
Refrigerated vehicles must be pre-cooled to the required range before loading. In the UAE summer (June to September), loading of chilled and frozen goods is done only at enclosed, chilled docks.

## 6. Equipment checks
Data loggers are calibrated every 12 months. Cold store doors and seals are checked weekly.
