# Inventory Cycle Count Procedure

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-WH-004 | **Version:** 2.2 | **Owner:** Inventory Control Manager

## 1. Purpose
Cycle counting replaces a full annual stock take. Small parts of the stock are counted every day so records stay accurate all year.

## 2. Count frequency (ABC classes)
| Class | Share of stock value | Count frequency |
|---|---|---|
| A | Top 80% of value | Monthly |
| B | Next 15% of value | Quarterly |
| C | Last 5% of value | Twice a year |

High-risk items (electronics, pharmaceuticals, alcohol and tobacco) are counted monthly whatever their class.

## 3. How to count
- Counts are blind: counters do not see the system quantity.
- Counts are done at the start of the shift before picking starts in that zone.
- Results are entered on the handheld scanner straight away.

## 4. Variances
- A variance within ±2% of quantity, and under AED 1,000 (£200) in value, is adjusted automatically.
- Larger variances need a recount by a different person within 24 hours.
- If the recount confirms the variance, the Inventory Control Manager investigates and must approve the adjustment. Adjustments over AED 20,000 (£4,000) also need the Site Director's approval.

## 5. Accuracy target
Location accuracy must be at least 99.5% each month. Results are reported to customers in the monthly service review.
