# Warehouse Health and Safety Procedure

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-HSE-002 | **Version:** 4.0 | **Owner:** HSE Manager | **Sites:** Jebel Ali DC, Dubai Industrial City DC, Daventry DC (UK)

## 1. Personal protective equipment (PPE)
Everyone on the warehouse floor must wear:
- high-visibility vest or jacket
- safety boots with toe protection
- cut-resistant gloves when handling strapping, pallets or blades

Hard hats are required in the racking aisles during replenishment and in the yard. Visitors are given PPE at reception and must be escorted at all times.

## 2. Forklift and material handling equipment (MHE)
- Only trained and licensed operators may drive forklifts, reach trucks or powered pallet trucks. Licences are renewed every 3 years.
- Operators complete a pre-use check at the start of every shift and record it in the MHE app. Any defect means the truck is tagged "Do not use" and reported to the Shift Supervisor.
- Speed limit inside the warehouse is 8 km/h, and 5 km/h in pedestrian zones and loading bays.
- Pedestrians must use marked walkways. Drivers must sound the horn at blind corners and when exiting aisles.

## 3. Racking inspections
- Racking is inspected visually every week by the Shift Supervisor.
- A trained inspector carries out a full racking inspection every 12 months.
- Damage is rated by a traffic-light system:
  - **Green:** minor, monitor at next check.
  - **Amber:** offload within 4 weeks and repair.
  - **Red:** offload immediately and isolate the bay until repaired.

## 4. Heat stress (UAE sites)
From 15 June to 15 September, outdoor yard work stops between 12:30 and 15:00 in line with the UAE midday break rule. Cool water and rest shelters must be available at every yard. Supervisors check on outdoor staff at least every 30 minutes.

## 5. Manual handling
Single-person lifts are limited to 25 kg. Anything heavier needs a two-person lift or lifting equipment.

## 6. Incident reporting
All incidents and near misses must be reported in the HSE portal before the end of the shift. Injuries needing first aid are reported to the HSE Manager within 1 hour. Serious injuries are reported to the HSE Manager and Site Director immediately, and the area is made safe and left undisturbed.
