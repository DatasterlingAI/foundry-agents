# Supplier and Carrier Onboarding Procedure

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-PROC-002 | **Version:** 1.9 | **Owner:** Procurement Manager

## 1. When this applies
Use this procedure before Meridian places any order with a new supplier, carrier or subcontractor.

## 2. Steps
1. **Request:** the requesting manager submits a New Supplier Request in the procurement portal, with the business reason.
2. **Due diligence:** Procurement collects:
   - trade licence or company registration
   - VAT registration certificate
   - bank details on company letterhead
   - insurance certificates (goods-in-transit at least AED 500,000 or £100,000; public liability at least AED 5 million or £1 million)
   - signed Supplier Code of Conduct
3. **Checks:** Finance verifies bank details by calling a known number (never a number from the same email). Compliance runs sanctions screening.
4. **Approval:**
   - spend under AED 250,000 a year: Procurement Manager
   - spend AED 250,000 to 1 million: Procurement Manager and Finance Director
   - spend over AED 1 million: also the Chief Operating Officer
5. **Set-up:** Finance creates the supplier in the ERP only after all approvals are recorded.

## 3. Carriers
Carriers must also provide fleet details, driver licence checks, and evidence of GPS tracking on all vehicles. New carriers start on a 3-month trial with monthly performance reviews against the Carrier SLA Policy (MFL-OPS-001).

## 4. Annual review
Insurance certificates and licences are rechecked every 12 months. Suppliers with expired documents are blocked from new orders until updated.

## 5. Typical timeline
Onboarding normally takes 10 working days once all documents are received.
