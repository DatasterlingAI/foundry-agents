# Customs and Shipping Documentation Guide

**Company:** Meridian Freight & Logistics (fictional)
**Document ID:** MFL-CUS-001 | **Version:** 5.0 | **Owner:** Customs Compliance Lead

This is an internal guide for Meridian staff. Always check current customs rules before shipping.

## 1. Documents needed for every international shipment
- **Commercial invoice:** seller, buyer, description of goods, HS code, quantity, unit value, total value, currency and Incoterm.
- **Packing list:** number of packages, weights and dimensions.
- **Transport document:** Bill of Lading (sea), Air Waybill (air) or CMR note (road).

## 2. Extra documents, when needed
- **Certificate of Origin:** needed when the buyer wants to claim lower duty under a trade agreement.
- **Export declaration:** needed for UAE exports and for UK exports above the value threshold.
- **Licences or permits:** needed for controlled goods, such as some chemicals and dual-use items.

## 3. HS codes
Every item must have a 6-digit HS code at minimum. Use the 8-digit UAE or 10-digit UK commodity code when shipping into those countries. Wrong codes can cause delays, extra duty or fines, so check with the Customs Compliance team if unsure.

## 4. Incoterms Meridian supports
| Incoterm | Who arranges main transport | Who pays import duty |
|---|---|---|
| EXW (Ex Works) | Buyer | Buyer |
| FCA (Free Carrier) | Buyer | Buyer |
| FOB (Free On Board, sea only) | Buyer | Buyer |
| CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight, sea only) | Seller | Buyer |
| DAP (Delivered at Place) | Seller | Buyer |
| DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) | Seller | Seller |

Meridian advises customers to avoid DDP into countries where they are not registered for VAT.

## 5. UAE free zones
Goods moving between Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA) and the UAE mainland need a customs declaration and are subject to UAE customs duty and VAT on entry to the mainland.

## 6. Timelines
Submit complete documents at least 48 hours before vessel cut-off for sea, and 24 hours before flight departure for air. Late or incomplete documents are the main cause of shipments missing their sailing.
